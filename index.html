How to reduce photo size to 20KB–50KB for SBI & IBPS exams?

Open the Banking Suite preset on OptiFile, select your passport photo, and click Format. Our engine automatically sets the dimensions to 200×230 pixels and locks the file size strictly around 38KB.

Yes! Inside any Exam Preset slot for Passport Photos (SSC, UPSC, Banking, or NTA), check the "Print Name & Date on Photo" option to add a crisp white nameplate strip at the bottom of your photograph.

Are my uploaded ID cards, photos, and signatures safe?

100% safe. OptiFile runs entirely on client-side HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly technology. Your photos, signatures, and PDFs never leave your phone or computer.
